Inhabitants is a comprehensive mod that enriches the world of Minecraft with new mobs. All creatures are made with attention to detail and are well integrated into the vanilla game. Here, you can find a lot to explore—from shy and cute critters to ferocious monsters to fight. Expand your gameplay with these mobs and have fun!

## Bogre

![Bogre image](https://i.imgur.com/TwBcZKz.png)

Bogres are a new species in mangrove swamp biomes. They enjoy loneliness and mostly avoid company. Players can serve Bogre with some ingredients to make strange food with interesting side effects!

Depending on the ingredient, food represents a special effects that can aid players in different situations. Despite its smell and look, it gives useful effects. Bogres can carve bones! Place 3 bone blocks in front of one to get a giant bone club. It's heavy—use a potion of strength to wield it.

I also heard that they are quite good dancers…

## Impaler

![Impaler image](https://i.imgur.com/vzalplG.png)

Impalers are spiky underground creatures that hunt below deepslate. They can see players from afar and run extremely fast. When threatened, they scream, dropping nearby stalactites. Beware of the spikes on their arm—they don't stay on for long. Defeat one to use its spikes to craft concussive arrows or powerful handheld drills. Explorers may notice that there are 4 different variants.

![border](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/e0a911d42883381b51631d08708b3e95b749fe3f.png)

## Warped Clam

![Warped clam image](https://i.imgur.com/SwDdLaW.png)

Warped Clams live in the End. They are peaceful and immobile. Step on a clam to launch yourself into the air—fun with Elytra! Clams occasionally grow Ender Pearls. Use a brush on the particles to collect one. Clams need time to produce more. Move a clam with a shovel; right-click to pick it up and place it anywhere.

![border](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/e0a911d42883381b51631d08708b3e95b749fe3f.png)

## Setup/Run
If you're familiar with Minecraft mods, you can download the mod jar from [Curseforge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/inhabitants) or [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/inhabitants). You'll need [Geckolib](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/geckolib) as well.

If you're unfamiliar with them, we recommend using a launcher like the [Curseforge App](https://www.curseforge.com/download/app) which manages your mods for you.
Create a new modpack and select Minecraft version 1.20.1 and Forge as the modloader. Add our mod "Inhabitants" and all the dependencies will be installed for you.
